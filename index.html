Narrative Silhouettes

Your profile becomes the shape and your words fill it. Everything happens in this browser tab. Your photo and writing are never uploaded.

1Photo

Best result: a side profile with the background removed in Canva, downloaded as a PNG with a transparent background. A profile against a plain, bright wall also works.

2Narrative

3Type & page

4Color

Turn on Fill to print the silhouette itself behind the words, then pick a text color that stands out against it. A background of None saves as transparent in the PNG and SVG; the PDF prints it on white. The caption uses the silhouette color when Fill is on. Outline draws a line around the shape and moves the text in so it never touches the line. In the SVG the outline is a real path, so a laser can cut or score along it.

5Save

The SVG keeps live text, so the laser software needs the same typeface installed. If it doesn't have it, engrave the PNG instead.

Your writing is kept as a draft in this browser so a refresh won't lose it. Photos are not kept.
